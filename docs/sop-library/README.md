# SOP and literature test set

A small test set for the SOP and literature library (plan 011) and digital SOPs (plan 012), chosen around the assays Wali's lab runs: ELISA, single-point compound screen with dose-response follow-up (CellTiter-Glo or HiBiT readout), enzyme kinetic screen, Dual-Glo reporter gene expression, and plasmid assembly (Gibson, Golden Gate) and purification.

`manifest.json` is the machine-readable index (one entry per item: source, license, format, local files, status, why, what it tests). `fetch.py` downloads anything still pending (`python fetch.py`, standard library only). `licenses/` holds the license texts of the GitHub-hosted code.

Status on 2026-09-29: the GitHub-hosted items are saved in this folder. The other 21 files were downloaded on Wali's laptop to `C:\dev\sop-library` with the same layout (this cloud environment's network policy blocks the source sites); `manifest.json` records their size and sha256. The Assay Guidance Manual chapter was downloaded by hand in a browser because NCBI serves a CAPTCHA to scripts. Items marked "pending" in the table below refer to this folder only.

In the repo: this index (`README.md`, `manifest.json`, `fetch.py`) plus the openly licensed and Creative Commons files: 12 SOPs under `sops/` (the CC BY 4.0 protocols.io, NEB and Thermo PDFs, and the two CC BY-SA / GNU FDL OpenWetWare pages) and 7 papers under `literature/` (six CC BY 4.0, plus the CC BY-NC-SA Assay Guidance Manual chapter, which is for internal, non-commercial testing only). Each matches the `sha256` in `manifest.json`. The three Promega manuals (CellTiter-Glo TB288, Nano-Glo HiBiT TM516, Dual-Glo TM058) are All Rights Reserved and stay in `C:\dev\sop-library` on Wali's laptop. The GitHub-hosted items (iGEM InterLab, LabOP Golden Gate, Opentrons serial dilution, PyLabRobot) are committed under `sops/`, with their MIT and Apache-2.0 license texts in `licenses/`.

## Licensing rules for this set

- **CC BY 4.0 / MIT / Apache-2.0:** free to use, including as repo seed data, with attribution.
- **Vendor, private use:** Promega manuals are All Rights Reserved. Keep them in this private folder for testing; do not commit them to the repo or seed data.
- **CC BY-SA / GNU FDL (OpenWetWare):** fine to use; anything derived and redistributed must stay share-alike.
- **CC BY-NC-SA (Assay Guidance Manual):** internal, non-commercial testing only.

## Gaps

- No openly licensed cellular HiBiT lytic protocol or 96-well Dual-Glo transfection protocol exists on protocols.io; the Promega manuals cover the readouts, and the DLR protocol covers an open reporter workflow.
- No open single-point screen at the classic 10 uM with Echo dispensing; the Flavivirus protease SOP (100 and 50 uM, Multidrop) is the closest well-parameterised one.
- No Assay Guidance Manual chapter is CC BY; the HTS Assay Validation chapter is kept under its non-commercial license.
- Verified but left out to keep the set small: BCA assay (doi 10.17504/protocols.io.6n5hdg6), Power SYBR qPCR (doi 10.17504/protocols.io.8epv5rxw6g1b/v1), BioLegend sandwich ELISA (doi 10.17504/protocols.io.98xh9xn), ALPK1 384-well HTS (doi 10.17504/protocols.io.n2bvjn74wgk5/v1).
